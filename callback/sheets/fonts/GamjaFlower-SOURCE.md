# Gamja Flower

Source: [Google Fonts / Gamja Flower](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/gamjaflower).

Copyright (c) YoonDesign Inc. Distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see `GamjaFlower-OFL.txt`.

`GamjaFlower-Regular.woff2` is a full-font WOFF2 conversion of the official `GamjaFlower-Regular.ttf`, produced with fontTools 4.68.0 and Brotli 1.2.1. No subsetting or glyph removal was performed. The conversion retains all 12,021 glyphs and all 12,020 Unicode mappings.

Original TTF: 12,615,444 bytes. WOFF2: 908,216 bytes.

This asset affects the display of digital callback records. Original uploaded photographs and stored record values are unchanged.
